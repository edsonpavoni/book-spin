Book Spin

Upload a book's covers, or several books. Get a transparent 3D book turning on its corner, light enough for any page (one 5 KB script, no libraries).

Books

Drop or click to replace an image; spine and back are optional. With more than one book, the book changes to the next one on the frame where only its pages face you. The demo: Art in the Age of Pixel Hallucinations and two mock covers from Sacred in Flux.

Page edges
Look

Spotlight: a low hard light from the left, a reflection sweeping across the covers and a cool rim on the edges, after the 2025 launch renders.

Backdrop

The HTML file holds everything (all the images inside), transparent background: open it, or drop it in an iframe. The embed uses your own image URLs.
