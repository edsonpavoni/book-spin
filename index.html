Book Spin

Upload a book's covers. Get a transparent 3D book turning on its corner, light enough for any page (one 4 KB script, no libraries).

Covers

Drop or click. Spine and back are optional.

Page edges
Light
you

Seen from above: drag the light around the book. The centre is straight overhead, the edge is low on the horizon. The shadow falls the other way.

The HTML file holds everything (images inside), transparent background: open it, or drop it in an iframe. The embed uses your own image URLs.
